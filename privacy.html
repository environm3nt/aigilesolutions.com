Privacy

This site collects exactly one kind of data: what you type into the contact form — your name, email address, and whatever you tell me about your project. That goes to my inbox (delivered via Web3Forms) and is used for one purpose: replying to you. It is never sold, shared, or added to a mailing list.

Site traffic is measured with Vercel Analytics, which is cookie-free and collects no personal identifiers. There are no tracking pixels and no advertising scripts anywhere on this site.
