What it replaces

A recruiter's morning: searching job boards, guessing which postings are real, looking up the company, hunting for the hiring manager on LinkedIn, checking whether a candidate they know fits, writing an email. Two to three hours a day per recruiter, most of it before the first conversation. The engine does it overnight and the recruiter starts at the conversation.
