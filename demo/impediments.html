Connected systems

The evidence trail is only as good as its inputs. The platform reads three systems and requires a reason on every blocked or overrun ticket.

Jira

Tickets, estimates, status transitions, and the comment left when a ticket is flagged blocked or misses its window.
Rule enforced
Blocked or overrun → a structured reason comment is required, naming whether the cause is internal, client, partner or external.

Slack

Delivery automation channel plus the shared client channel: blocked notifications, deadline changes, competing priorities, outage announcements.
Signals captured
Deadline moved · priority swap · partner sandbox status · production incident

GitHub

Pull request state and review latency. A PR waiting on review past threshold becomes an impediment on the linked ticket.
Threshold
Review requested and idle > 2 business days

Classification

Each impediment is classified by an LLM pass over the ticket comments and linked Slack thread, then attributed. Low-confidence classifications are queued for a human check.
